# OctoAcme — Requirements Traceability & Acceptance Criteria Checklist

## Purpose
Help Business Analysts, Product Managers, and QA Leads ensure requirements are clearly documented, traceable to delivered features, and validated through acceptance criteria.

## How to Use
Maintain this as a living document during planning and execution. Review at sprint planning, mid-sprint refinement, and before release. Link to the project backlog and QA test plan.

## Acceptance Criteria Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to validate each requirement before it enters the sprint backlog.

- [ ] Requirement is written from the user's perspective (user story format recommended)
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are specific, measurable, and testable
- [ ] Acceptance criteria cover the happy path and known edge cases
- [ ] No ambiguous terms (e.g., "fast", "easy", "good") without measurable thresholds
- [ ] Stakeholder and Product Manager have reviewed and agreed
- [ ] QA Lead has confirmed the criteria are testable
- [ ] Business Analyst has mapped criteria to at least one test case or test type
- [ ] Requirement is linked to a project goal or success metric

## Requirements Review Checklist (pre-sprint)

- [ ] All new requirements in the upcoming sprint have acceptance criteria defined
- [ ] Requirements have been reviewed by Product Manager and Business Analyst
- [ ] QA Lead has confirmed requirements are testable
- [ ] Developers have reviewed requirements and raised any clarifying questions
- [ ] Requirements are linked to backlog items (issues / user stories)
- [ ] Traceability matrix updated for new requirements
